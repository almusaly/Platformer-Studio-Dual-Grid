Platformer Tileset Assignment

Design two tilesets and build a platform with them in Platformer Studio below. You can use Photoshop, Affinity, or GIMP.

Requirements

  • 2 tilesets of your own design (for example: grass and stone).
  • Each tileset is a 96 × 96 pixel PNG: a 3 × 3 grid of 32 × 32 tiles (corners, edges and center).
  • Use both tilesets to build one platform.
  • Submit one PNG image of your platform.

Steps

  1. Design each tileset on a 96 × 96 canvas and export it as a transparent PNG.
  2. In the studio below, click the upload button next to Tilesets and add both PNGs.
  3. Click a tileset, then drag on the grid to draw. Corners and edges are placed for you.
  4. Check that the edges look right. Fix your tiles and use Replace to test again.
  5. Click the Export button (picture icon, top right), then Download PNG, and submit that image here.
